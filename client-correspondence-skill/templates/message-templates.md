# Message Templates — Client Correspondence

Ready-to-adapt skeletons. Always adapt wording/facts to the actual situation — never send
verbatim without checking names, dates, and approved facts.

---

## T1. WeChat — warm acknowledgment (casual-formal)
**When:** replying to a client's personal/friendly note; no decisions involved.

CN:
> 稻垣先生，您好！收到您的回复很高兴。其实…，完全没有问题，请不必有压力。……我们慢慢来，不着急。那我们9月7日（周一）见，北京时间上午10:30，日本时间11:30，期待和您交流！

EN:
> Hi Mr. Inagaki, thanks for your message — so glad to hear from you! …no worries at all. …let's take it slowly. See you on Monday, Sep 7, 10:30 Beijing time (11:30 in Japan). Looking forward to it!

---

## T2. Group chat — formal numbered confirmation (F)
**When:** client lists points and asks for confirmation; bosses present in GC.

CN:
> 稻垣先生，您好！三点均已收到，确认如下：
> 1. 交流语言：按此前商定的方式执行即可，没有问题。
> 2. 交流平台：同意以微信作为主要联系方式。重要内容（如报价单、会议纪要等）我方会整理成单独的文件后通过微信发送，方便保存与查阅。
> 3. 定期会议：非常赞同。可按每季度至少一次的频率安排面对面或网络会议，具体时间与议题每次提前沟通确定即可。
> 以上如无其他问题，期待周一的会议。谢谢！

EN:
> Dear Mr. Inagaki, thank you for your message. All points are confirmed as follows:
> 1. Language: as previously agreed — no issues.
> 2. Platform: WeChat is agreed as the main channel. Important content (quotations, meeting minutes, etc.) will be compiled into single files and sent via WeChat for easy saving and reference.
> 3. Regular meetings: fully agreed, at least once a quarter, face-to-face or online, with the specific time and topics confirmed each time.
> Looking forward to the meeting on Monday. Thank you!

## T3. Group chat — welcome a newly added member (bilingual)
**When:** a new person (client side) is added to the GC. Provide CN + EN when unsure whether
the reader reads Chinese.

CN:
> 田中先生，您好！欢迎加入我们的沟通群。我是圣诺亚的宫（Angelina），今后如需要日常沟通或翻译支持，请随时联系我，请多关照！

EN:
> Hello Mr. Tanaka, and welcome to the group! I'm Angelina from SUN-NOA. Please feel free to reach out to me anytime for daily communication or translation support. Looking forward to working with you!

---

## T4. Internal note to boss — summarizing a client message + asking how to proceed
**When:** a client message needs a decision the user cannot make. Send privately, NOT in the GC.

CN:
> 领导，您好！刚才稻垣先生在我们的群里发来几点/以下事项：1）…；2）…；3）…。
> 另外，…（新情况，如新成员加入）。
> 请问这几点由您直接回复，还是需要我先拟一版回复给您确认？谢谢！

EN:
> Hi, I wanted to flag the following from [client]: 1)… 2)… 3)…
> Also note: [new development].
> Would you like to reply directly, or shall I draft a reply for your confirmation?

## T5. Formal email — cover letter structure
**When:** sending documents (quotation, minutes, TDS/SDS) by email.

EN structure:
> Dear Mr. Inagaki,
> Thank you for your message. Please find attached [document] as requested / as agreed.
> A brief summary is provided below for your reference: [1-2 lines].
> Should any point require clarification, please feel free to let us know.
> Kind regards,
> Angelina Gong
> Beijing SUN-NOA Technology Development Co., Ltd. | Tel: … | [signature]

CN structure:
> 尊敬的稻垣先生：
> 您好！根据我方沟通/贵司要求，现将[文件名称]整理如下并随邮件发送，请查收。
> 简要说明：[1-2行]。
> 如需进一步说明，欢迎随时联系。
> 顺祝商祺！
> 宫（Angelina）｜北京圣诺亚科技发展有限公司

## T6. Polite deferral / not-yet-approved (F)
**When:** the user cannot yet commit (leadership not confirmed), or the company is not ready
to answer.

CN:
> 感谢您的信任与建议。相关内容我方需要先与内部确认，确认后会尽快回复您，请您见谅。

EN:
> Thank you for your trust and your suggestion. This will need to be confirmed internally
> first; we will reply as soon as it has been. We appreciate your patience.

---

## T7. Asking questions on behalf of leadership (GC, bilingual)
**When:** bosses ask the user to raise questions to the client in the GC.

CN:
> 稻垣先生、田中先生，您好！
> 我方管理层想借此机会与贵司交流以下几个问题。提前发出，方便双方准备，我们也可以在周一的会议中详细讨论：
> 1. ……
> （问题逐条列出，每条后附英文）
> 以上问题如方便，可在会前简单反馈；详细内容留待会议讨论。谢谢！

EN:
> Dear Mr. Inagaki and Mr. Tanaka,
> Our management would like to take this opportunity to discuss the following points with
> you. We are sending them in advance so that both sides can prepare, and we can discuss the
> details in the meeting:
> 1. …
> Please feel free to reply briefly before the meeting if convenient; the details can be left
> for discussion. Thank you!
