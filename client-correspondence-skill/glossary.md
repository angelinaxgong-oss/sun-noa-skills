# Glossary — CN⇄EN Phrase Library & Business Vocabulary

This file has two jobs:
1. **CN⇄EN phrases by intent** — the "mimic library" for building natural Chinese business
   messages (and their English equivalents). Pick formulas per intent; never translate the
   English side into Chinese word-by-word.
2. **Business vocabulary** — terms used in SUN-NOA's correspondence (adhesives, woodworking,
   trade).

---

## 1. Phrase Library by Intent

### 称呼与问候 Openings
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| WeChat/GC opening | 稻垣先生，您好！ | Dear Mr. Inagaki, hello! / Hi Mr. Inagaki, |
| Email opening (formal) | 尊敬的稻垣先生：您好！ | Dear Mr. Inagaki, |
| Opening reply | 您好！感谢您的回复。 | Thank you for your message / reply. |
| Welcome new person | 田中先生，您好！欢迎加入我们的沟通群，今后请多关照。 | Hello Mr. Tanaka, and welcome to the group! Looking forward to working with you. |

### 感谢 Thanks
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Simple thanks | 谢谢！ | Thank you! |
| Warm thanks | 非常感谢您的回复/安排/支持。 | Thank you very much for your reply / for arranging this / for your support. |
| Acknowledging receipt | 已收到，谢谢。 | Received with thanks. |
| Deeply appreciated | 您的回复我们十分感谢。 | …it is much appreciated. |

### 确认与同意 Confirming / Agreeing
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Agree (no objection) | 我方均无异议，可以按此执行。 | Our side has no objection, and it can be arranged as proposed. |
| Numbered confirmation | 三点均已收到，确认如下：1)… 2)… 3)… | All three points are confirmed as follows: 1)… 2)… 3)… |
| Confirm arrangement | 就按我们之前商定的执行。 | As previously agreed. |
| Confirm file sending | 重要文件会整理成单独文件（Word/PDF）后通过微信发送。 | Important documents will be compiled into single files and sent via WeChat. |
| Confirm cadence | 可按每季度至少一次的频率安排。 | They can be arranged at least once a quarter. |

### 请求 Requests
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Polite request | 如方便，烦请… | Could you kindly…? |
| Asking for confirmation | 麻烦您提前确认，非常感谢。 | We would appreciate your confirmation in advance. |
| Inviting input | 欢迎随时告知/提出意见。 | Please feel free to share your thoughts at any time. |
| Asking who/how | 请问贵司一般通过什么流程处理？ | May we ask how this is normally handled on your side? |

### 委婉反馈 / 提出敏感话题 Softening / Sensitive topics
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Frame as feedback | 借此机会转达一些市场反馈：… | We would also like to share some market feedback: … |
| Price competitiveness | 成本相对国内同类产品偏高，价格竞争力有限。 | The cost is relatively high compared with equivalent domestic products, which limits price competitiveness. |
| Ask their view | 想了解贵司对此的看法。 | We would be glad to hear your views on this. |
| Raise an idea | 有一个想法想与贵司探讨：… | There is an idea we would like to explore with you: … |

### 承诺与后续 Follow-up promises
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Will follow up | 我方确认后会尽快回复。 | We will reply shortly once confirmed internally. |
| Will send later | 整理好后会发送给您。 | The documents will be sent to you once compiled. |
| Will prepare | 我们会提前准备相关资料。 | The relevant materials will be prepared in advance. |
| After meeting | 会后我们会发送会议纪要。 | The meeting minutes will be sent after the meeting. |

### 说明权限（下级在群内/无权决定时） Authority & hierarchy
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Needs internal confirmation | 此事需与领导确认后再答复您。 | This will need to be confirmed internally before we can reply. |
| Boss will reply | 相关事项将由我方领导确认后回复。 | Our management will confirm and reply directly. |
| Take offline | 价格方面的细节建议会后单独沟通，您看可以吗？ | It may be better to discuss the pricing details separately — would that be acceptable? |

### 收尾 Closings
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| WeChat/GC closing | 期待周一的会议。谢谢！ | Looking forward to the meeting on Monday. Thank you! |
| Warm closing | 期待与您的交流！ | Looking forward to our discussion! |
| Email closing (formal) | 顺祝商祺！ / 此致敬礼 | Kind regards / Best regards |
| Thanks in advance | 提前感谢您的支持！ | Thank you in advance for your support! |

### 致歉与延迟 Apologies / delays
| Intent | 中文 | English |
|---|---|---|
| Apology | 非常抱歉，回复晚了。 | Apologies for the late reply. |
| Inconvenience | 给您带来不便，敬请谅解。 | We apologize for any inconvenience caused. |
| Delay | 因…原因，预计将延迟至… | Due to…, this is expected to be delayed until… |
