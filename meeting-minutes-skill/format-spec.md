# Meeting Minutes — Format Specification (.docx)

## Page
- A4; margins top/bottom 1134 twips, left/right 1134 twips
- Fonts: English Arial; Chinese 宋体 (SimSun)

## Structure & sizes
- Title (e.g., "Oshika 会议纪要 / Meeting Minutes") — 16pt bold, centered
- Meeting info block (date/time, platform, attendees, minutes by) — 10.5pt
  - Use the attendee naming rule in SKILL.md; give CN line then EN line.
- Section headings (一、沟通机制 / Communication) — 13pt bold
- Body — 10.5pt; numbered items as CN paragraph then EN paragraph below
- Bold key terms (公司名称, 型号, dates) where helpful

## Content rules
- CN paragraph first, EN paragraph directly beneath (grey not required; plain text is fine).
- Numbered items restart per section (1, 2, 3…).
- Action items as bulleted lines; use owner + deadline where known.
- Closing line (both languages) at the end.
- Keep to 1–2 pages when possible; no images unless the user asks.

## Header/footer (optional)
- Header: none required; Footer: page number right-aligned, 9pt.
