## Notes for the drafter
- Keep statements factual and neutral; attribute only when it adds value.
- Anything uncertain → mark "待确认 / to be confirmed" and tell the user before sending.
- Leadership review is required before the minutes are posted or emailed.
- Deliver as a single .docx file.
- CN attendee list: leaders are addressed as 总 (宫总、吴总（沈阳）、吴总（北京）) — never 先生 for leaders; 吴总（沈阳） comes FIRST whenever he attends; non-leaders keep their normal forms (先生/女士/name).
