# Meeting Minutes (bilingual CN/EN)

## Purpose
Turn raw/quick notes plus context into polished bilingual minutes, using the structure approved by SUN-NOA leadership (see templates/minutes-template.md). Default output is a single .docx file, CN and EN together — ready to send via WeChat/email per the client-file agreement.

## Required inputs
1. Meeting topic / type (client call, quarterly review, visit, etc.)
2. Date, time, platform (e.g., WeChat / Zoom / Teams / face-to-face)
3. Attendees on both sides — follow the naming rule below
4. The rough notes (messy is fine — they will be sorted)
5. Optional: agenda or previously agreed points to recap

## Attendee naming rule
- CN leaders (bosses): surname + 总, with location in parentheses to distinguish — 宫总、吴总（沈阳）、吴总（北京）. ONLY leadership uses 总; never apply it to non-boss attendees.
- CN others: keep existing style (given name / 先生 / 女士) with role in parentheses — e.g., 王女士（负责日常与订货）、李女士（Linda Li，翻译）、宫女士（Angelina，口译/记录）.
- EN: Mr./Ms. + Surname; role in parentheses. Leaders stay Mr./Ms. unless a specific title is known and confirmed.
- Order: leadership first, by seniority — 吴总（沈阳）is ALWAYS first in the attendee list whenever he is present, then 吴总（北京）, then 宫总 — then other attendees by role. If the seniority order among leaders is unclear, ask the user.
- Example (Oshika meeting): 我方 SUN-NOA: 吴总（沈阳）、吴总（北京）、宫总、王女士（负责日常与订货）、李女士（Linda Li，翻译）、宫女士（Angelina，口译/记录） / EN: Mr. Wu (Shenyang); Mr. Wu (Beijing); Mr. Gong; Ms. Wang (daily operations & ordering); Ms. Li (Linda Li, interpreter); Ms. Gong (Angelina, interpretation & minutes)

## Workflow
1. Collect the required inputs; ask for anything missing (never guess attendees, dates, or facts).
2. Sort the notes into the standard sections WITHOUT inventing content: meeting info → key arrangements/announcements → discussion by topic → decisions → action items → next meeting.
3. Ambiguous notes: if a note is unclear, ask the user, OR keep the wording conservative and flag the item for user confirmation before finalising.
4. Produce CN + EN pairs (CN first, EN below), using the minutes glossary terms; mirror the structure between languages.
5. Action items must have an owner and (where known) a deadline. If notes do not state who does what, ask.
6. Decision gate: minutes speak for the company — leadership must review before the minutes are posted in a group chat or emailed. Do not post without this confirmation.
7. Deliver the .docx (single file, per the client-file agreement), bilingual.

## Section template (standard)
1. 会议信息 / Meeting Information — date/time, platform, language used
2. 参会人员 / Attendees
3. 重要安排 / Key Arrangements (e.g., contact-person handover, upcoming visits)
4. 议题讨论 / Discussion by Topic (numbered; keep factual)
5. 决定事项 / Decisions (if any)
6. 后续行动 / Action Items (owner + deadline)
7. 下次会议 / Next Meeting

Closing line (both languages):
- CN: 如有遗漏或出入之处，欢迎随时指出，我们将及时补充修订。
- EN: Should anything be missing or inaccurate, please feel free to let us know, and it will be revised promptly.

## Rules
- Never invent prices, dates, commitments, or statements not present in the notes.
- Keep the discussion neutral and non-attributive unless attribution adds value (e.g., "noted by Mr. Wu, Shenyang").
- If leadership decisions are pending on a topic (e.g., OEM), record it as "to be confirmed / 待确认" rather than as agreed.
- Omit unnecessary fluff; minutes are dense but readable.
